Submission data validation

Starting up. The first visit downloads Python and the libraries into your browser, which takes about half a minute on a normal connection. After that it is cached and opens quickly.

Everything runs on your own machine. Documents you add are read in this tab and are never uploaded.

Try it yourself with demo data

Nothing here is real. Every firm, person, client and figure in these files is invented, so you can open them and look around freely.

  1. Download the six files below. Four are law firm submissions; the other two are the spreadsheets the app checks them against.
    submission 1 submission 2 submission 3 submission 4 the counts to check the reference list
  2. Drag the four submissions into the first box at the top of the page, the one asking for .docx files.
  3. Add the two spreadsheets to the two boxes on the right: the counts to check goes in the Power BI box, and the reference list in the Staff Portal box.
  4. Read the table. Green means the app counted something different from the spreadsheet and the green figure is its answer. Amber means it could not count that one and wants a person to look. You can type over any figure, and the colours follow.
  5. Optional. Open "Staff Portal row order" and paste these three lines in, to see the app check the batch against the page it came from: 
    Commercial property: Thames Valley
EU and competition
Banking and finance

Two of the four submissions are deliberately the same one twice, an earlier version and a later one. The app keeps the later and sets the earlier aside, which is why four files produce three rows.
